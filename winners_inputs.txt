The song's accompanying music video shows Beyoncé performing in a series of dance sequences. It earned three awards at the 2003 MTV Video Music Awards, and its director, Jake Nava, received the Music Video Production Association award for Best R&B Video in 2004. Since 2003, "Crazy in Love" has remained a fixture in Beyoncé's live performances and concert tours. The American Society of Composers, Authors and Publishers (ASCAP) named "Crazy in Love" one of the most performed songs of 2004. Artists such as David Byrne have recorded their own versions of the song, and it has featured in a variety of television programmes and other media.

In 1992, Acen sampled the title song "You Only Live Twice" for his track "Trip II the Moon Part 2." Icelandic singer Björk recorded a cover version in 1997. The following year, Robbie Williams drew on the song's distinctive string figure for "Millennium," though for reasons of cost it was re-recorded rather than sampled directly from the film. Coldplay performed the song during their 2001 tour, and Natacha Atlas covered it for her 2005 compilation album The Best of Natacha Atlas. Shirley Bassey, who is credited with three original Bond themes, has likewise covered the song.

A great deal of Sholay was filmed in the rocky landscape around Ramanagara, a town near Bangalore in Karnataka. To reach the sets more easily, the filmmakers had to construct a road linking the Bangalore highway to Ramanagara. Art director Ram Yedekar erected an entire township on the location. A prison set was also built outdoors near Rajkamal Studio in Mumbai so that its natural lighting would match that of the on-location shoots. For a time, one section of Ramanagara was known as "Sippy Nagar" in honor of the film's director. As of 2010, tourists passing through Ramanagara were still offered a visit to the "Sholay rocks," where much of the film was shot.
